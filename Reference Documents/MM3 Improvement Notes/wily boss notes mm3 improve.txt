Wily 1 boss notes:
Main boss:

256B5: Total number of turtles for battle, not recommended to edit at all
25758-5B: Time from when it starts moving until the next Kamegoro is released (4 bytes)
2575C-5F: Which way Kamegoro moves after being defeated (4 bytes) - 
not recommended to edit
25760-65: Boss life bar that decreases for each turtle defeated
2577E: Y position to place turtle below the boss, when released -  not recommended to edit
2578D: HP of turtle (default: 03)

From here, the next three items are the values for each turtle.
There seems to be 6 bytes, but the last one seems unused
257D6-DB: Initial movement direction
257DC-E1: Lower digit of X/Y movement speed (slow half)
257E2-E7: Upper digit of X/Y movement speed (fast half)
;--------------------------------------
Turtle:

257F3: Initial value of time until shell is removed
However, it will not be removed unless the Y coordinate is 25810 or more
25810: Shell can be removed at Y axis coordinates above this value
25814: If shell is not removed within the time, the time until it can be removed again

2593E: Y axis coordinate of the next water surface after coming out of the water once
The following four items are 6 bytes each for the speed when Kamegoro comes out of the water surface
25942-47: Lower digit of Y axis speed (slow half)
25948-4D: Upper digit of Y axis speed (fast half)
2594E-53: Lower digit of X axis speed (slow half)
25954-59: Upper digit of X axis speed (fast half)

25963: When moving downwards, this Y coordinate is used to determine that it is underwater
25987: Same as 25963, these 2 should be the same value
259E3: Y-axis position from turtle of water splash
25A26: HP of shell
;--------------------------------------
Turtle's shell:

25A4D: Lower digit of Y-axis speed (slow half)
25A52: Time from appearance to stopping
25A57: Time to explosion 1
25A64: Sound effect when dealing damage
25AA8: Turnaround time when moving up and down after stopping
25ACF: If it is 02 or higher, it will explode after 25A57 has passed and 25AD3 has been counted
25AD3: Time until explosion 2
25ADF: Time until explosion 2 will start flashing when it reaches this time
;--------------------------------------
Water vortex tornados (little cross shaped sprite to init):

25AF6: Time from appearance to start
25B36: Time from when the vortex appearance notice is given until it appears
25B64: Y-axis position of appearance notice when the vortex appears from below
25B7D-7E: X-axis position when appearing from left/right (the one with the notice)
25B7F-80: Direction when appearing from left/right
25B81-90: Y-axis position when appearing from left/right 16Byte
25B91-A0: X-axis position when appearing from below 16Byte
25BFE: Y-axis position of the vortex appearance from the notice of appearance of the vortex from below
25C10: Upper digit of the X and Y axis speed of the vortex (fast half)
25C18-19: 2 bytes left and right of the X axis position where the vortex will emerge from the vortex appearance notice
;--------------------------------------
Water vortex tornados:

25C2A: Y axis position where the vortex from below disappears
25C5C: When it appears from the left or right, does it affect up to this X axis distance from the main body?
25C63: The Y axis distance above
25C7E: Lower digit of the X axis speed at which megaman is swept away when affected by the vortex appearing from the left or right (slow half)
25C82: Upper digit of the above (fast half)

Wily 2 boss notes:
Yellow Devil:

240A1: Time until starting battle  after lifebar is filled (first blocks appearing) - this is 1-time only - default: FF (4.15 seconds)
240D7: X position to spawn blocks from (left side) - not recommended to edit - this is 1-time only at start of battle
24105: X speed of blocks (fast half only) - cant edit slow half
2413C: unknown timer (left side)
24141: Number of times to shoot bullet (left side)
241B4: Speed of bullets (slow half)
241BB: Speed of bullets (fast half)
241D4: Time until next bullet after the 1st
241EA: Time until starting next block movement phase (after final bullet)
242C6: Base Y position to place the stacks of vertical blocks ontop of each other, when it breaks apart (left side only)

caution: certain values might cause unexpected results such as more blocks spawning then intended
242ED: Vertical speed of these vertical stacked blocks when breaking apart left side of screen only - (fast half speed)
24338: Time until next stack of blocks, not understood but can cause interesting delays, seen in some other romhacks.
24353: X position where the eyeball appears (right side)
24358: unknown timer (right side)
2435D: Number of times to shoot bullet (right side)
2444F: Time until begin bullet shoot   (right side)

244F5: Jump height Y speed of bouncing blocks (slow half)
244FA: Jump height Y speed of bouncing blocks (fast half)
244FF: X speed of bouncing blocks (slow half)
24504: X speed of bouncing blocks (fast half)

this is only on the final bounce after the first few ones
2450A: Jump height Y speed of bouncing blocks (slow half)
2450F: Jump height Y speed of bouncing blocks (fast half)
24514: X speed of bouncing blocks (slow half)
24519: X speed of bouncing blocks (fast half)
